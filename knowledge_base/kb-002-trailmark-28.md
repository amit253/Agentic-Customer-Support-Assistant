title: Trailmark 28 backpack
---

The Trailmark 28 is a 28-liter travel backpack with a clamshell opening,
removable hip belt, padded laptop sleeve, and water-resistant recycled nylon.
It is a standard item unless a customer adds a personalized embroidered patch.

The backpack should be returned in unused condition with its original tags and
packaging when the applicable return policy permits a return.
