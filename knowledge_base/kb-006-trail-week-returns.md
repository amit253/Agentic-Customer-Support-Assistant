title: Trail Week extended return window
---

For qualifying standard items ordered from September 1 through September 30,
2026, Northstar Gear extends the return deadline to November 15, 2026. This
campaign rule supersedes the normal 30-day deadline for those orders.

The extension does not apply to personalized or embroidered final-sale items,
and it does not approve a refund. The order must still be unused and meet the
standard condition requirements.
