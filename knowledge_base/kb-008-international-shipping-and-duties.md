title: International shipping and duties
---

International customers are responsible for import duties, taxes, and customs
charges assessed by the destination country. Those duties and outbound shipping
charges are not refundable under the Northstar Gear policy.

If an eligible item is returned, any refund amount must come from a confirmed
order/refund record. Support must not estimate the amount from the item price.
