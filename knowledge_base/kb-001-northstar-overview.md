title: Northstar Gear support overview
---

Northstar Gear is a fictional travel-gear company. Support can explain product
features, shipping policies, return eligibility, warranty coverage, and how to
reach a human specialist.

Support may read confirmed order facts and policy facts. Support cannot invent
an order status, approve a refund, promise a refund amount, or promise a
delivery date that the order record has not confirmed.
