title: Final-sale items and warranty claims
---

Personalized or embroidered items are final sale for change-of-mind returns.

Northstar Gear may inspect a manufacturing defect under the 12-month limited
warranty. A warranty review can result in repair, replacement, or another
resolution determined after inspection. It is not an automatic refund and must
not be described as one before confirmation.
