title: Standard return policy
---

Unused, non-personalized items may be returned within 30 calendar days of the
recorded delivery date. The item must be in original condition with tags and
included accessories.

The 30-day rule is the default. The Trail Week document defines a narrower,
date-qualified exception for qualifying orders placed during September 2026.
Personalized final-sale items remain excluded from both windows.
