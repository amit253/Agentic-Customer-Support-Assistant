title: CityHaul 20 backpack
---

The CityHaul 20 is a 20-liter commuter backpack with a structured laptop
compartment and weather-resistant zippers. It is a standard item unless it was
personalized.

Product questions about capacity and features can be answered from this
document. Return timing is governed by the standard or Trail Week policy,
not by the product description.
